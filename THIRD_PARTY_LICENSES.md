# Third-party vocabulary data

Kana Biyori includes a curated bilingual subset of JLPT vocabulary data adapted from [Ringoo Open Vocabularies](https://github.com/ringooai/open-vocabularies), specifically the N5/N4/N3 English and Traditional Chinese CSV files.

Source data is under the **MIT License**. Curated local entries and corrections may differ from the original. Original source: https://github.com/ringooai/open-vocabularies
